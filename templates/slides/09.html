IPv6: the same job, more space

IPv4

32-bit addresses

192.168.50.10

ARP on the local Ethernet link.

IPv6

128-bit addresses

2001:db8::10

Neighbor Discovery instead of ARP.

  • IPv6 has multicast, but no broadcast.
  • Dual stack means using IPv4 and IPv6 together.
  • A link-local address does not prove internet connectivity.
