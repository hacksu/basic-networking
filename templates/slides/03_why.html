Why do OSI and TCP/IP exist?

OSI: organize the responsibilities

A shared reference model for designing and describing communication systems.

It gives engineers common terms for where a function or failure belongs.

TCP/IP: connect different networks

A protocol suite with agreed message formats and behavior.

It lets different devices and networks exchange traffic through IP.

Layering lets an application use Ethernet or Wi-Fi without implementing each link technology itself.
