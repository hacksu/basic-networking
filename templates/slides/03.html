Hub, switch, router

Hub · layer 1

Repeats signals to other ports.

Shared bandwidth and collision domain.

Switch · layer 2

Learns source MAC addresses.

Forwards frames within a VLAN.

Router · layer 3

Uses IP routes to choose a next hop.

Connects different IP networks.

An unmanaged switch is still a switch. A home router often combines several functions.
