When does TCP or UDP make sense?

TCP: completeness and order

You need an ordered byte stream, with loss recovery handled by the transport.

Examples: SSH sessions, many file-transfer and database protocols.

UDP: application-controlled messages

You need datagrams and can handle loss, ordering, and timing in the application or a higher-level protocol.

Examples: live voice/media and game-state updates.

Freshness can matter more than an old update. DNS can use either transport; HTTP/3 adds QUIC’s reliability over UDP.
