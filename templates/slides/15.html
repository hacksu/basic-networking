  • A typical initial DHCPv4 exchange is called DORA.
  • A lease can supply an IPv4 address, mask, gateway, and DNS servers.
  • The host renews its lease; the settings are not necessarily permanent.
The wired demo uses static settings. The GL.iNet router will show the DHCP-configured case.
