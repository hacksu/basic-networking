What does /24 actually mean?

Host A: 192.168.50.10/24 · Mask: 255.255.255.0

DestinationNetwork with this /24Decision for A
192.168.50.20192.168.50.0/24On the local subnet
192.168.51.20192.168.51.0/24Needs a route beyond the subnet
  • The first 24 bits identify this network; eight bits remain.
  • Here, .0 is the network address and .255 is the broadcast address.
  • A looks at its routes to choose an outgoing interface and next hop.
This example uses a /24. Matching the first three octets is not a general rule for every prefix length.
